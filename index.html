Rainbow Schools · UK & Punjab, India

Every child deserves a place
to be seen, taught, and believed in.

We're building Rainbow Centre Punjab — a holistic, inclusive learning and vocational centre for disabled and orphaned children — and developing new resources like the Punjabi Reading Scheme to teach and celebrate Punjabi language and culture.

"A world where every child, regardless of ability, lives a happy, safe and fulfilled life." — Rainbow Schools, our vision
Since 1999

Founded on a family's own journey.

Founders Arvinder Singh Paul and Sandeep Kaur Paul have visited centres across India supporting disabled and orphaned children — inspired by their own daughter, Gurleen. Rainbow Centre Punjab is their answer: a purpose-built, research-led centre designed to be a model for inclusive education across India.
