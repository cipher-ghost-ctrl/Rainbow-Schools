Our story

Founded on a family's own journey.

Since 1999, founders Arvinder Singh Paul and Sandeep Kaur Paul have visited centres across India supporting disabled and orphaned children — inspired by their own daughter, Gurleen. Most centres they saw provided shelter, but few offered a truly holistic, inclusive education.

Rainbow Centre Punjab is their answer: a purpose-built, research-led centre designed to be a model for inclusive education across India — eco-friendly, therapy-integrated, and open to the wider community.
