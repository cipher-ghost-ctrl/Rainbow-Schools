Get involved

Three ways to help build this.

Whether you've got ten minutes, a weekend, or a whole community behind you — there's a way to help Rainbow Centre Punjab become real.

🤝

Volunteer

Teaching, therapy, fundraising, design or social media — there's a role to fit your skills. Get in touch to find out what's needed right now.
📣

Fundraise

Become a Friend of Rainbow Schools and run an event in your community — see the ambassador programme below.
💛

Donate

100% of donations go directly to Rainbow Centre Punjab. Bank transfer also available on our Contact page.
