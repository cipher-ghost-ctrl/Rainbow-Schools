Who we support

Every child deserves to be seen, not sidelined.

We support disabled and orphaned children across Punjab, India, and their families — through direct care, and through a free advice service open to anyone who needs it.

The reality in India

In many communities, disability still carries deep stigma — some families are told their disabled child is a curse, or hidden away out of shame rather than supported. Rainbow Schools exists to change that: to give these children an education, a community, and a future.

Free parent advice

Rainbow Schools offers a free online advice service for parents of children with disabilities — wherever you are, whatever questions you have. Get in touch and we'll do our best to help, point you toward the right support, or simply listen.
