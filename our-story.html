Our story

From a fact-finding trip in 2009, to a foundation stone in 2019.

Ten years of visits, small beginnings, setbacks and progress — the real, unedited history of how Rainbow Centre Punjab came to be.

Timeline

The journey so far.

December 2009

Fact-finding trip to India

The first documented trip — visiting centres for disabled and orphaned children across India, including a moving visit to Pingalwara, to understand what already existed and what was still needed.

September 2010

Research trip

A further research visit to deepen understanding of the centres, needs, and gaps across the region.

March 2011

Small beginnings, finally starting…

The earliest real steps toward what would become Rainbow Schools — small, but the true starting point.

March 2012

Parent workshops at 8 centres in Northern India

Workshops delivered directly to parents across 8 different centres — sharing practical support and strategies.

October 2012

Rainbow Schools charity formed

The moment it became official — Rainbow Schools registered as a charity, ready to formalise years of visits and groundwork.

Milestone
April 2013

Play parachute games at Mildmay Adult Centre, Ilford

UK-based community engagement — bringing play and connection to a local adult centre.

April 2013

Supporting different centres in India

Continued work extending support across multiple centres, not just one location.

July 2013

East London Charity Bike Ride

A community fundraising ride through East London — an early example of the kind of grassroots events that now form the Friends of Rainbow Schools programme.

September 2014

Planning our next trip to India

Looking ahead to the next stage of visits and support work.

December 2014

Sensory Integration

A deeper look at sensory integration therapy — one of the specialist approaches informing how Rainbow Centre Punjab is being designed.

September 2016

Visited special needs schools in Delhi, Punjab and Pingalwara

A significant multi-city visit, deepening the research behind what Rainbow Centre Punjab would need to be.

September 2016

Inspiring thoughts…

Reflections from the ground — the people and moments that kept the mission going.

September 2016

Positive, negative… are we a battery cell?

A personal, reflective piece on energy, motivation, and what it takes to keep a cause like this moving forward.

May 2017

Land for a Rainbow School — bought

The breakthrough moment: 3 acres secured on the GT Road in Fatehgarh Sahib district — the essential first step for everything that followed.

Milestone
7 December 2019

Foundation Stone Laying, 3rd November 2019

The foundation stone officially laid — attended by local dignitary MLA Kuljit Singh Nagra. The most recent major milestone in the story so far.
