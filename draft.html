Home / Draft: UX Ideas

📝 Draft — internal proposal, not part of the live site nav User experience

Ideas to make the site feel real, not just correct.

The structure and content are solid now — this is about trust, emotional connection, and getting people to actually act. Grouped below by area.

Biggest single fix

Real photos, anywhere.

The whole site currently runs on emoji/icon placeholders — no real photos of the land, the family, the advisory board, or the children being helped. For a charity asking people to donate, this outweighs almost everything else below. Even a handful of real photos would move the needle more than any other item here.

Trust & emotional connection

Making people believe this is real.

High impact

Social proof

A donor count, a short quote from a supported family, or the Advisory Board's real photos once confirmed. People give to people, not descriptions of people.

Nice to have

Newsletter signup

On Contact or the homepage — captures interest from people not ready to donate today but who might be later.

Learn Punjabi specific

Making the scheme feel alive.

High impact

Audio for letter sounds

The guide itself stresses "say the sound, not the letter name" — right now that relies entirely on a parent reading text aloud. Even simple text-to-speech per letter would be a genuine upgrade, not just polish.

High impact

Save progress

Closing the tab currently loses your gold stars and place in the book. A returning learner picking up where they left off is a basic expectation for something book-like.

Nice to have

Completion badges

A simple certificate or badge per Stage — kids respond well to a tangible "I finished this" moment.

Donation conversion

Keeping the ask present, not pushy.

Nice to have

Sitewide funding indicator

Not just the homepage arc — something subtle sitewide (e.g. in the footer) so the ask never fully disappears without feeling pushy.

Nice to have

Interactive First Phase breakdown

Each line item as its own mini progress bar rather than static text, so people can see exactly what their money would fund.

Accessibility & reach

Especially relevant for this charity.

High impact

Skip-to-content & focus states

Currently missing site-wide. Matters both for genuine accessibility and because this is a disability charity — makes it more than a checkbox.

Nice to have

Respect reduced-motion

The scroll-reveal and flipbook page-turn animations should turn off for anyone who's set that system preference.

Nice to have

Punjabi/English toggle

Worth considering for the main charity site itself (not just Learn Punjabi), given the audience it's trying to reach.
