About Rainbow Schools

Founded on a family's own journey.

Since 1999, founders Arvinder Singh Paul and Sandeep Kaur Paul have visited centres across India supporting disabled and orphaned children — inspired by their own daughter, Gurleen.

Most centres offered shelter. Few offered a real education.

On their visits across India, Arvinder and Sandeep saw the same pattern again and again: centres that could keep disabled and orphaned children safe, but rarely ones equipped to genuinely teach them, include them, or help them thrive.

Rainbow Centre Punjab is their answer — a purpose-built, research-led centre designed to be a model for inclusive education across India: eco-friendly, therapy-integrated, and open to the wider community, not just those who live there.

The founders

Arvinder & Sandeep Paul

Arvinder Singh Paul

Co-founder

Born in Punjab, India, and moved to London, UK at age 8. A retired Primary School teacher and teacher trainer, with a lifelong passion for Punjabi, Sikhi and special needs education. Also author of the Rainbow Punjabi Accelerated Reading Scheme.

Sandeep Kaur Paul

Co-founder

Co-founded Rainbow Schools alongside Arvinder, inspired by their daughter Gurleen. Together they've spent over two decades visiting and supporting centres for disabled and orphaned children across India.
